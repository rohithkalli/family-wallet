# 💰 Family Wallet

> An AI-powered family finance management application designed to help families organize, track, and understand their finances in one place.

## 🎯 Problem

Managing expenses across a family can become difficult when spending information is scattered across different people, payments, and records.

Family Wallet aims to provide a centralized experience for managing family financial information.

## 💡 Product Idea

Family Wallet focuses on making family finance management:

- Simple
- Organized
- Easy to understand
- Accessible from one place

## ✨ Key Features

- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family-focused financial management
- 💰 Expense tracking
- 📊 Budget and spending management
- 🤖 AI-powered financial assistance
- 📱 Android application experience
- 🔐 Secure handling of user information

## 🧠 Product Management Perspective

### Target Users

Families and individuals who want a simpler way to organize and understand shared financial activity.

### User Problem

Users need a convenient way to keep financial information organized instead of managing it across multiple places.

### Product Goal

Build a simple, AI-assisted financial management experience that helps users make better use of their financial information.
